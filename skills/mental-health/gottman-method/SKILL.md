# Gottman Method — Relationship Communication Tools

Evidence-based tools drawn from John and Julie Gottman's research on relationship stability and communication.

## When to Activate This Skill

- The user is talking about a conflict with a partner, family member, or close friend.
- Communication patterns feel stuck, repetitive, or escalating.
- The user wants tools to repair or strengthen a relationship.
- Emotional flooding is present during conflict.

## The Four Horsemen (Warning Patterns)

Identify and name these when present — clearly but without shaming.

| Horseman | What It Looks Like | Antidote |
|---|---|---|
| **Criticism** | Attacking character: "You always…", "You never…" | Soft startup: "I feel… when… I need…" |
| **Contempt** | Eye-rolling, mockery, superiority, sarcasm | Build culture of appreciation; express admiration |
| **Defensiveness** | Counter-attack, deny responsibility, play victim | Own your part, even a small part |
| **Stonewalling** | Shutting down, withdrawing, going silent | Physiological self-soothing; timed break |

> **Contempt is the single most corrosive pattern.** Name it explicitly when present. It must stop.

## Soft Startup Formula

Convert a complaint into a request:

**"I feel [emotion] when [specific situation]. I need [positive request]."**

- NOT: "You never listen to me."
- YES: "I feel unheard when I'm talking and you're on your phone. I need us to have phone-free time during dinner."

## Repair Attempts

Small actions that de-escalate during conflict:
- "Can we take a break and come back to this in 20 minutes?"
- "I'm sorry I said that. Let me try again."
- "I hear you. I can see why that bothered you."
- "I love you even when we're having this fight."
- Touch, humor (when safe), or a brief acknowledgment that things got heated.

## Emotional Flooding Protocol

When someone is flooded (heart rate > 100 bpm, tunnel vision, reactive):
1. Name it: "It sounds like you've reached your limit right now."
2. Recommend a timed break: at least 20-30 minutes, with a specific return time.
3. During the break: DO NOT ruminate. Physical activity, music, a walk.
4. Return to the conversation calmer — not defeated, calmer.

## Love Maps and Positive Sentiment Override

For longer-term relationship health:
- **Love Maps** — Do you know your partner's current world? Their stresses, dreams, worries?
- **Turning toward bids** — Notice small emotional bids (comments, jokes, sighs) and respond to them.
- **Positive sentiment override** — Relationship bank account. Five positive interactions for every negative.

## What to Avoid

- Do not take sides.
- Do not label either person as "the problem."
- Do not encourage surveillance or controlling behavior.
- Do not suggest repair actions that require the other person to change first.
